Features

Everything your film deserves

Built for people who shoot film and want to remember every roll — without spreadsheets or paper notes.

Log every roll

Film stock, camera, format, frame count, shooting month, lab and notes — saved in seconds.

Scans linked, not copied

Add scans straight from Apple Photos. Originals stay where they are, without duplicates filling your iPhone.

Browse your way

Search everything, or group your rolls by date, film, camera or location to find any frame fast.

Film & camera library

Popular stocks from Kodak, Fujifilm, Ilford and more are built in. Add your own films and cameras with covers.

Shooting statistics

See your most-shot films, favorite cameras, places and activity over time — tap any stat to see its rolls.

Contact sheets

Turn a roll into a classic contact sheet, then save it to Photos or share it anywhere.

Places on a map

Pin where each roll was shot and revisit your photo walks on the map.

iCloud Sync

Your rolls sync through your private iCloud and come back after reinstalling or on a new iPhone.

Your language, your look

Available in English, Tiếng Việt and 한국어, with light and dark appearance.

How it works

From loaded roll to archived memories

Three simple steps, from the moment you load the film to the day your scans come back.

STEP 01

Load a roll

Pick the film and camera, set the date and format. FiLog remembers the details you'd otherwise forget.

STEP 02

Add your scans

When the lab sends your scans, add them from Apple Photos and drag frames into the right order.

STEP 03

Relive & share

Browse full-screen, add notes and places, check your stats and share a contact sheet.

Privacy first

Your rolls stay yours.

FiLog has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your data lives on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud — the developer never sees it.

Read the Privacy Policy

  • Originals stay in Apple Photos
