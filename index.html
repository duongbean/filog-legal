Getting help

Please include your FiLog version, iPhone model, iOS version, what you were doing and whether it also happens offline. For sync issues, include the status shown in FiLog Settings and whether Photos access is limited or full. Remove private notes, locations, account details and images from screenshots before sending them. Never send passwords.

Photos are missing

FiLog links to originals in Apple Photos instead of copying them. Check that the original still exists and that FiLog can access it. In FiLog, open Settings > Sync Photos to check and repair photo links, or relink an unavailable frame. Finding photos on another iPhone also requires iCloud Photos.

Sync or restore is pending

Check that you are signed in to iCloud, have iCloud storage available and are online, then reopen FiLog and check the sync status in Settings. Roll information that synced earlier can be restored with the same Apple Account. FiLog syncs roll information, not original photo files, older in-app photo copies or custom cover images.

Please don't delete FiLog, clear its storage or delete its iCloud data to troubleshoot. Those actions can remove data that exists only on your device. Contact support first.

Privacy and data controls

In FiLog Settings you can manage permissions, turn iCloud Sync on or off and review storage. Deleting a frame in FiLog never deletes the original in Apple Photos. Deletions sync to your other devices. Turning sync off does not erase data already in iCloud.
