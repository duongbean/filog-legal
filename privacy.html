FRAME 01

About FiLog

FiLog helps you organize film rolls and link scanned photographs from Apple Photos. It does not require an account and contains no advertising or third-party analytics. FiLog can use Apple's iCloud to sync your information when iCloud Sync is available and turned on. The developer does not operate any server and does not receive your data.

FRAME 02

Stored on your iPhone

Roll details, film and camera selections, dates, notes, lab information, optional shooting locations, photo links and frame order. Also your own film and camera entries, cover images you choose, preferences and temporary photo previews. Older in-app photo copies, if present, remain in the app's local storage.

FiLog keeps a local history of earlier values after sync changes so data can be recovered, so deleting a roll does not immediately erase every earlier local copy.

FRAME 03

Photos and permissions

FiLog asks for Photos access when you add or repair photo links. You can choose limited or full access and change it any time in iOS Settings; limited access can prevent FiLog from finding photos outside your selection.

FiLog links to originals in Apple Photos rather than uploading or duplicating them. It creates temporary previews for display and files for sharing or export. Choosing a custom cover copies that image into the app.

Removing a roll or frame never deletes the original from Apple Photos. FiLog asks to save a contact sheet to Photos only when you choose that action.

FRAME 04

Location

When you choose to use your current location, FiLog asks for access only while the app is in use. Coordinates, place names and addresses you save can be attached to rolls or frames. Maps, address search and place lookup use Apple system services, which may process the map area, coordinates or search terms needed to provide them. FiLog never tracks your location in the background.

FRAME 05

iCloud Sync and recovery

When iCloud Sync is on, FiLog sends roll details, your own film and camera entries, notes, dates, locations, photo links and frame order to your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can access. FiLog keeps sync progress and an account identifier on your device so different accounts' libraries are never mixed. Syncing happens while FiLog is open; offline changes wait until you are back online.

FiLog does not upload original photos, older in-app photo copies or custom cover images. Apple Photos and iCloud Photos manage your originals separately — photo links are not photo backups. Recovery uses only information that synced beforehand, with the same Apple Account, and finding originals requires that they still exist and that FiLog has Photos access. Recovery is therefore not guaranteed.

FRAME 06

Sharing

Photos and contact sheets are shared only when you choose to share or export them. The app or service you share to handles that content under its own practices. Exported images may contain photo metadata such as location, so review it before sharing. Information you send to support is used only to answer your request.

FRAME 07

Your choices and deletion

Edit or delete rolls and frames, manage Photos and location access, and turn iCloud Sync off in Settings. Turning sync off stops future syncing but does not remove data already in iCloud. Deletions sync to your other devices, and FiLog keeps deletion markers so deleted items do not come back.

Deleting the app removes its local data, covers and in-app photo copies. It does not delete your originals in Apple Photos or data already in iCloud. To delete FiLog's iCloud data, go to iOS Settings › your Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage. This affects all your devices and cannot be undone. FiLog has no separate account to delete.

FRAME 08

Children, security and changes

FiLog is not directed at children and collects no personal information on any server. It relies on iOS data protection and Apple's iCloud security; no system can guarantee complete security. If FiLog's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
